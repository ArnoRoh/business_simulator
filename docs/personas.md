# Personas

> **Status:** first draft, unreviewed, and **not yet validated against real users.**
> These are constructed from the project owner's operating context, not from field
> research. Treat them as hypotheses. Blocked on
> [Q-001](../memory/OPEN_QUESTIONS.md) for which is primary.

Personas here exist to settle design arguments, not to decorate a deck. Each one names
the constraint it puts on the product.

---

## Learners

### Grace — the operator with traction

Mid-thirties. Runs a food-processing business in Arusha with six employees, four of them
casual. Registered, not fully compliant. Has a real product and repeat customers. Was
approached by a supermarket chain and could not meet their supplier requirements.

**Her constraint is not knowledge in general** — it is that she does not know what the
certification actually requires, what it costs, how long it takes, or whether the
contract would cover it.

- Android phone, 3GB RAM. Data bundle she monitors.
- Reads English competently; thinks in Swahili. Business vocabulary mixed.
- Time comes in fragments — twenty minutes between problems.

**Design constraints she imposes:** resumable in short sittings; concrete numbers rather
than principles; content on formalisation and standards must be *specific* or it is
worse than useless to her.

**She is the strongest candidate for primary user.** The background note points at firms
with existing traction and verifiable bottlenecks.

### Joseph — the experienced operator who has not started

Late forties. Fifteen years as a production supervisor at a larger manufacturer. Deep
technical knowledge, knows the industry's suppliers and buyers, has managed people. Wants
to start his own operation. No business plan, no pitch, no polish.

**On the note's evidence he is among the best candidates a programme could find, and
the one a business-plan competition would filter out first.**

- Uses a smartphone for calls, WhatsApp and mobile money. Not app-fluent.
- Would find a game-like interface unfamiliar and possibly undignified.

**Design constraints he imposes:** the hardest and most important ones. If the tool
measures screen fluency, Joseph scores badly and the instrument is broken
(`assessment.md`, "Fairness"). Needs a facilitator-assisted mode. Tone must not be
childish.

### Amina — the young founder

Twenty-four. Graduate. Selling online, small revenue. Fluent with apps, comfortable
presenting, has attended two entrepreneurship workshops. Applies to everything.

**She is the person who currently wins business-plan competitions**, and the note is
sceptical that she is the best use of public money.

- Newer Android, better connectivity. Will finish anything she starts.
- Will work out what the tool rewards and give it that.

**Design constraints she imposes:** the gaming problem. If Amina can reverse-engineer
the scoring, the instrument is compromised. She is the adversarial test case, and the
reason indicator logic is not published.

### Peter — the livelihood consolidator

Fifties. Runs a duka. Steady, modest, supports a household. Not trying to build a
company and does not want to.

**Peter must be served without being condescended to.** A tool that treats him as a
failed transformational entrepreneur has failed — this is [Q-006](../memory/OPEN_QUESTIONS.md)
made concrete.

**Design constraints he imposes:** the trajectory choice must be real, with genuine
content behind livelihood consolidation, not a lesser branch.

## Programme side

### Faith — programme officer

Runs selection for a grant programme. Three hundred applications, funding for twenty,
two weeks to decide. Currently reads business plans and scores them against a rubric she
privately doubts.

**She is the consumer of the readiness profile.** Her constraint: she needs something
she can defend to a board, quickly, that is not just a number — and her process has
rules about what evidence she may consider.

**Design constraints she imposes:** the profile must be skimmable and defensible; no
composite score (`assessment.md`); must fit a real institutional process.
[Q-002](../memory/OPEN_QUESTIONS.md) exists to find the real Faith and ask her.

### Daniel — facilitator

Runs training for a local BDS provider. Twenty people in a room, mixed literacy, shared
devices, unreliable power.

**Design constraints he imposes:** group and assisted modes; offline; printable
material; content that survives being taught by someone who did not write it.

### Ruth — funder / evaluator

Assesses whether the programme worked. Has read the same literature the background note
draws on and is sceptical of self-reported outcomes.

**She is the internal check on overclaiming.** Anything we say about what the tool
measures should be written as if Ruth will read it — because the standard the note
applies to MbeleNaBiz is the standard she will apply to us.

## Notes

- **Not validated.** These are hypotheses from the owner's context. Field research
  should confirm or replace them, and the session that does it should record what
  changed.
- Grace and Joseph are the design centre for capability; Joseph alone is the design
  centre for accessibility.
- Amina is the adversarial case. Peter is the dignity case. Neither is an edge case.
