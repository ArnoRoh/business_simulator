## Context

What situation forces a decision? What constraints apply? Include the constraints that
are external and non-negotiable, and say where they come from.

Enough that a reader in a year understands the problem without reconstructing it.

## Decision

What we are doing, stated plainly and in the active voice. "We will …"

## Consequences

**What this gets us.**

**What this costs us.** Be honest. A decision with no downside was not a decision.

**What it forecloses.** Options that become expensive or impossible.

## Alternatives considered

For each: what it was, what was attractive about it, and the specific reason it lost.

An alternative dismissed in one line was not seriously considered — and the next
contributor will propose it again.

## Revisit if

The concrete condition that should reopen this. Not "if circumstances change" — a
specific trigger someone could actually notice.
