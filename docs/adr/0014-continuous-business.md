# ADR-0014 — Propose one continuous business

**Status:** Proposed

**Date:** 2026-09-23

**Deciders:** Project owner; awaiting review of the proposed design

**Related:** [Design proposal](../MV-BS-DES-001-continuous-business.md),
[ADR-0007](./0007-four-chapter-arc.md),
[ADR-0012](./0012-play-first-business-journey.md),
[ADR-0013](./0013-short-introduction-and-preserved-chapters.md)

## Context

The owner rejects both the cumbersome older interactions and the shallow five-day
introduction. Links to four separate chapters do not solve this. The current task asks
for design before implementation, with freedom to replace the game structure.

## Proposed decision

Use the linked design as the candidate for review: one business with continuous cash,
stock, orders, staff and obligations. Progress comes from operating consequences and
player goals. Separate simulated events, intentions, reported activity and verified
real activity. Preserve existing learner records and their historical meanings.

Acceptance would supersede the four-chapter structure in ADR-0007 and the experience
structure in ADR-0012/0013. It would require a new record contract and a review of the
portal's chapter-based intake. This proposal does not change a deployed programme gate,
authorise funding decisions or establish learner acceptance.

## Consequences

Continuity gives earlier choices a material role in later problems. It also increases
the range of reachable states. Financial reconciliation, recovery paths and understandable
causality will require testing across those states. The design gives up broad sector
coverage in favour of repeated, connected decisions in one business.

## Alternatives considered

Retain a short introduction plus separate chapters: preserves current content, but the
owner has rejected its shallow entry and weak continuity. Combine the old turn lists:
reuses work, but carries forward the interaction and sequencing problem. Add a large
open-world simulation: offers breadth, but adds controls, content and testing without
demonstrating that the core decisions are enjoyable.

## Revisit if

Players cannot explain later consequences of their earlier choices, routinely need
facilitator direction to continue, or encounter repeated business states with no useful
decision. Review the product setting if local operators cannot recognise its economics.
